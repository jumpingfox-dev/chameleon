# Chameleon 1.1.3 (build 5)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
HDR plays as HDR, and Dolby Vision plays as Dolby Vision on TVs that support it.
New screensaver with four styles to choose from and customize, or have the screen dim instead.
No more on-screen Back and Close buttons on TV; the remote's Back does the job.
The seek bar now uses your theme's color.
Fixes for the age rating's position and for keeping the screen on during videos.
```

## Full changes

**Playback**
- HDR10, HLG and HDR10+ play as HDR: the hardware decoder now draws straight onto a video
  surface of its own instead of a Flutter texture (which is SDR only), so the TV switches into
  its HDR mode. Dolby Vision profile 8 files without a Dolby Vision decoder play as HDR10.
- A second video engine, ExoPlayer, plays Dolby Vision files as Dolby Vision on devices with a
  Dolby Vision decoder. It's also the backup when mpv can't open a file, and mpv is ExoPlayer's
  backup. Settings › Playback › Video engine can choose Automatic, Always mpv, or ExoPlayer
  whenever it can play the file.
- The screen stays on while a video plays.
- The seek bar's played part is the theme's color; its handle only shows while the bar has
  focus or is dragged.
- The age rating stays lined up with the title logo whether the controls show or not.

**Screensaver**
- New Settings › Screensaver tab with four styles: Backdrops, Poster wall, Clock and Floating
  logo, each with its own options, plus when it starts, how long it keeps the screen on, and
  how much to dim it. A Preview button tries it straight away.
- Set to Off, the screen dims after a chosen time instead.
- Never shows during a video. Any button or touch brings the app back without doing anything
  else.
- New accounts on a TV start with the Floating logo; phones start with Off (dimming).

**TV and desktop**
- No on-screen Back or Close buttons on TV detail pages; focus starts on Play. Detail popouts
  on desktop have no Close button either (Esc or a click outside closes them).
- Phones held sideways in the player keep their normal size instead of being shrunk.
- Screen types (phone, tablet, desktop, TV) are decided in one place (`ScreenType`).

**Under the hood**
- Plain `http://` servers are allowed for ExoPlayer, which goes through Android's networking.
- Quieter logs: media_kit's video output messages and routine notices are no longer printed.
