# Chameleon 1.1.10 (build 12)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
Chromecast plays again with newer Jellyfin servers, subtitles included.
Previews and converted streams work with newer servers too.
With the menu pinned, Cast is in it alongside Settings and your profile.
Pages now fill the whole screen on tablets, top to bottom.
The top bar disappears completely as you scroll.
```

## Full changes

**Newer Jellyfin servers**
- Newer servers (Jellyfin 12) refuse the sign-in key under its old name in an address, which
  stopped Chromecast streams and subtitles, card video previews, and the player's converted
  streams and external subtitles. The key now goes under both names, so old and new servers
  both accept it.

**Menu and top bar**
- With the menu pinned, Cast (when there's somewhere to cast to) sits with Settings and your
  profile at its foot, with the same cast symbol; Home's top bar then goes entirely.
- The top bar fades as well as slides away, so nothing of it is left at the edge.

**Tablets**
- Pages run under the status bar and the home indicator rather than stopping short of them,
  leaving no blank strips at the top and bottom (Home, detail pages, Settings, ...). Scrolling
  content still keeps room at its ends; the library A–Z column stays clear of the home
  indicator.
