# Chameleon 1.1.11 (build 13)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
A roomier top bar on phones: the logo sits in the middle, and Settings and your profile are in the menu.
```

## Full changes

**Phones**
- The top bar's logo is centered, like page titles.
- Settings and your profile (picture and name) are at the foot of the slide-out menu instead
  of the top bar; choosing your profile closes the menu, then opens "Who's watching?". Cast
  stays in the bar, when there's somewhere to cast to.
- The bar's buttons sit a little further in from the screen's rounded corners.

Tablets, TVs and computers are unchanged.
