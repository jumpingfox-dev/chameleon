# Chameleon 1.1.8 (build 10)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
Sign in by picking your profile, add more servers, and open straight to whoever used the app last.
Previews show a quick stretch of the title, or its own trailer.
Settings are simpler, in clear sections.
Title pages: centered names, every collection a movie is in, selectable media info.
My Media shows your libraries' artwork.
Chromecast no longer closes the player while loading.
Lots of layout fixes on TVs and phones.
```

## Full changes

**Signing in and switching**
- Sign-in connects to the server first, then shows its users to pick from (or a username
  and password if it lists none), with Quick Connect and Change server.
- "Who's watching?" is a full screen, from your profile picture too: one row that scrolls,
  those signed in on this device first (marked, with yours as Current), then the server's
  other users. Picking someone signed in switches straight away; anyone else goes to their
  password, on the same server. Add account goes straight to a new username and password.
- The app opens as whoever used it last, however many accounts are saved.
- More than one server: Settings › Server › Servers lists them (add, switch, remove), and each
  server's people only ever show under it.

**Pages**
- Every page but Home has a back arrow and its name centered in the top bar (TVs too).
- The top bar hides while scrolling down and returns at the top of the page; on phones the
  page then fills the screen.
- Movie pages list every collection the movie is in, above More like this; actor pages list
  theirs too. Media info can be selected with the remote.
- Actor pages use their best-rated title's backdrop, blurred. Backdrops fade only at the bottom.
- My Media shows each library's artwork, sized like the other thumbnails.
- Home's thumbnails show 3½ across on wider screens, and posters about 6.

**Previews**
- A card's preview is the title's own preview video or trailer (a light copy, silent), or a
  quick flip-book of its scrubbing thumbnails from a quarter of the way in (a show's pilot).
  Titles with neither keep their artwork. The featured banner no longer previews.

**Settings**
- Grouped into sections, each in one box: Appearance (with the screensaver), Account,
  Playback (Episodes, Streaming quality, Seeking & resuming, Subtitles, Audio, Video,
  Previews), Server, SyncPlay, About.
- assets/defaults.json is laid out the same way.

**Casting**
- The cast icon is always the familiar one, and the cast menu scrolls with many devices.
- The player no longer closes while a Chromecast loads; if it can't play something, it says
  so and carries on here.

**Fixes**
- Trickplay previews no longer show several thumbnails in one frame.
- Library pages' A–Z column no longer overflows as the top bar moves.
- The top bar comes back on pages only just long enough to scroll.
- Fonts are fetched by name; none are bundled any more.
