# Chameleon 1.1.5 (build 7)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
Trailer previews: rest on a movie or show and its trailer plays right in the card and the
featured banner. Turn them off or tune them in Settings › Playback.
New Trailer button on movie and show pages.
Shows say "Continue Watching" and open on the season you're up to.
Collections of shows list the shows, not every episode.
Leaving the app now pauses whatever is playing.
Genres has its own place in the sidebar.
```

## Full changes

**Trailer previews**
- After a moment on a card (2 seconds by default), its trailer plays: the server's own
  trailer when there is one, otherwise the title's trailer on YouTube (in YouTube's player,
  with its branding). Not on phones, and not in Continue Watching or Next Up.
- On TVs, posters and thumbnails on Home are the same height. A thumbnail plays its trailer in
  itself; a poster widens to 16:9 in its place, toward the screen's middle near the edge. The
  focus border steps aside while the trailer plays.
- In grids (libraries, search), the trailer plays in a panel over the card.
- The featured banner plays its trailer too, with nothing over it, and waits for it to finish
  (up to 3 minutes) before moving on, then holds for the usual interval.
- Settings › Playback › Previews: on or off, the delay, sound, and YouTube trailers. Settings
  and About link YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

**Detail pages**
- A Trailer button on movies and shows, played in the app.
- Shows: Play reads "Continue Watching" once you've started, and the page opens on the season
  of your next episode.
- Collections list what's in them (a collection of shows lists the shows).

**Everywhere**
- Leaving the app (Home button, another app, screen off) pauses playback, trailers and
  previews. Audio no longer keeps playing after you exit.
- Genres is a single link in the sidebar, above Libraries.
- Thumbnail cards sit the same distance apart as posters; a row's first card grows away from
  the sidebar when focused, and its last one away from the edge.
- No more empty space under the last section on TVs.
- Moving left quickly along a row no longer jumps to the sidebar, and only one of the banner's
  buttons is ever highlighted.
