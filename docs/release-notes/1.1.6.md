# Chameleon 1.1.6 (build 8)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
Redesigned movie, show and actor pages: full backdrops, collections, More like this and media info.
Hold Select on any title for Play, Play from beginning, favorites, mark played and more.
Continue Watching and Next Up play right away.
Cards preview with stills instead of trailers.
TV: text fields only open the keyboard when selected, and sign-in is easier.
TrueHD titles that wouldn't play now do.
```

## Full changes

**Detail pages**
- Movies, shows and collections open as their own page. The backdrop fills the page, fades
  into the sidebar, and scrolls more slowly than the page.
- Movies show the collections they're in (each a row, its title a link to the collection),
  More like this, and media info: video, audio and subtitle tracks, format, size and bitrate.
- Shows list their episodes, then their collections and More like this.
- Rows run to the screen's edge and move like Home's.
- Actor pages match: a large portrait, born (age) and where, died, how many of their titles
  you have, the biography, then rows of their movies and shows, over a softened backdrop
  from their best-rated title.
- Pages start on Play (an actor's on their first title) with a remote or keyboard.
- Play reads "Continue" when you're partway through a movie or a show.
- Favorite, Trailer and Shuffle are filled, so they're readable on any backdrop.
- Trailers play full screen from the Trailer button.
- The Back button shows on phones only; computers go back with Esc or the mouse's back button.

**Cards and Home**
- Hold Select (or long-press, or right-click) on a movie, show, episode or collection: Play
  or Continue, Play from beginning, More info, Add to favorites, Mark as played, and in
  Continue Watching and Next Up, Remove from the list.
- Continue Watching and Next Up play straight away when selected.
- Resting on a card shows stills from the title, fading from one to the next for up to
  10 seconds, instead of a trailer. The featured banner does the same, with its details
  still showing. Blurry or blank chapter images are skipped.
- Posters and thumbnails are the same height on TVs, and a poster widens to 16:9 for its
  stills, in rows and in library grids.

**TV**
- The remote moves over text fields without opening the keyboard; Select opens it. On
  sign-in, the keyboard's ✓ moves to the next field and signs in from the password, and the
  page moves up so you can see what you type.
- The logo is smooth on TVs that drew it with jagged edges.
- The screensaver can turn the TV off after a while (Settings › Screensaver).
- Backdrop screensaver: clock top right, logo bottom left, genres, and a fade between pictures.

**Fixes**
- Titles whose main audio track can't be decoded on the device (TrueHD on some TVs) play
  with another track instead of failing.
- The app no longer sometimes stays on the splash screen at start.
- Leaving the player right after it opens no longer loses your place.
- The Quick Connect window is narrower and easier to read.
