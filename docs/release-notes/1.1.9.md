# Chameleon 1.1.9 (build 11)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
Every season has its own page: its poster, Play, Favorite, Mark as watched, cast and episodes.
Mark movies, shows and seasons as watched in one press.
Favorite seasons and episodes, and find them in Favorites.
A new Genres section for Home.
A lighter top bar: buttons on glass, out of the way as you scroll.
With the menu pinned, Settings and your profile are in it.
Smoother scrolling with a remote.
```

## Full changes

**Seasons**
- A show's seasons are a row of posters on its page; each opens a page of its own over the
  show's backdrop: the season's poster, Play (or Continue), Favorite, Mark as watched, its
  description, the cast and its episodes.
- Mark as watched on movies, shows and seasons (every episode at once), shown straight away.
- Favorites lists seasons and episodes too, after movies, shows and collections, each named
  with its show. Episodes are favorited from the menu when you hold Select.

**Home**
- A Genres section: ten genres picked at random, as tiles like the Genres page's. New layouts
  include it; add it to yours from Edit home.
- My Media's tiles are the size of the other thumbnails.

**Top bar**
- Its buttons sit on frosted-glass islands, with the page showing all the way up behind them.
  Detail pages have no title in it (the title is big on the page itself).
- It floats over the page, so hiding and showing it no longer moves what's on screen; the logo
  and page titles show only at the top of a page. Going back to a page finds the bar as you
  left it.
- With the menu pinned, Settings and your profile are at its foot, and Home's bar goes when
  there's nothing left in it.

**Detail pages**
- Collection and More like this rows match Home's card sizes.
- No sliver of the backdrop below its fade.

**With a remote**
- Pressing Up quickly no longer brings the top bar down in the middle of a page.
- On library and genre pages the focused card keeps clear of the screen's edges, last row
  included; Home's Genres row scrolls like the other rows.
- Favorites brings a card's caption into view with it.
