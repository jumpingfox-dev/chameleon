# Chameleon 1.1.7 (build 9)

## Google Play "What's new" (en-US, under 500 characters)

```
A new look: a top bar, a menu that slides out (or stays open on big screens), and a bottom bar on phones you can customise.
Download movies and episodes to watch offline on phones and tablets.
One cast button for Chromecast and AirPlay.
Settings reorganised into sections.
Actor pages redesigned.
Holding Select on a TV now opens a title's menu.
Fixes for the iPad menu, Back, Genres and more.
```

## Full changes

**New layout**
- A top bar on every screen: the menu button, Back, and the page's name or the logo; casting,
  Settings and who's signed in on the right. It slides away as you scroll down and comes back
  at the top of the page.
- The sidebar is now a menu: Home, Search, Downloads, Favorites, Genres, your libraries under
  My Media, and Edit home. It slides out over the page as frosted glass, or on TVs, computers
  and tablets held sideways can be pinned open beside the page.
- Phones (and tablets held upright) have a floating bottom bar: Home, Search, Downloads and
  Movies by default, changeable in Settings › Appearance › Bottom bar (up to four).
- Home has a My Media row of your libraries. Pages sit evenly between the screen's edges.
- Back on any page goes Home before leaving the app; pressing the page you're on takes it back
  to its start. A genre opened from Genres goes back to Genres.

**Downloads (phones and tablets)**
- Download a movie or episode from its page, its row, or by holding it, and watch it with no
  connection. Downloads carry on in the background, with progress in a notification.
- The Downloads page shows what's downloading (pause, resume, cancel) and what's saved, by show.
- Where you get to while offline is sent to the server once it can be reached. Your Jellyfin
  account needs downloading allowed.

**Casting**
- One cast button for Chromecast and, on iPhone and iPad, AirPlay, in one menu.

**Settings**
- Grouped into sections (Appearance, Account, Playback, ...), each group opening on its own.

**Detail pages**
- Actor pages are laid out like a title's: their photo (selectable, shown full size), name and
  details over a wall of their posters, then their movies and shows.

**Fixes**
- The iPad's menu couldn't be opened by touch.
- Holding Select on a TV remote opened a title's menu and then picked its first option.
- The end of pages could sit behind the phone's bottom bar.
- No tooltips on buttons.
