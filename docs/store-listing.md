## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Chameleon is a Jellyfin client for Android TV, Google TV, phones and tablets, built to look great and play everything your server has to offer.

It's one app for every screen: a remote-friendly layout on the big screen and a touch layout on your phone, with the same look and the same settings on both. And the features other Jellyfin apps keep behind a subscription or a one-time unlock are all included here, free, with no ads and no paywall.

To use Chameleon, you'll need your own Jellyfin server.

With Chameleon, you can:

• Play movies and shows in HDR10, HLG and HDR10+, and Dolby Vision on devices that support it
• Send Dolby Digital, Dolby Atmos, DTS and TrueHD to your receiver or soundbar untouched
• Make the app yours with eight themes, a custom theme from any two colors, any Google Font for headings, text and subtitles, and five icon styles
• Build your own home screen: add, remove and reorder sections like Continue Watching, Next Up, Recently Added, Suggested For You, Because You Watched, your favorite genres and collections
• Skip intros, recaps and credits automatically or at the press of a button
• Scrub with thumbnail previews, and jump through any library with the A–Z bar
• Let the next episode play on its own with Up Next, with an "Are you still watching?" check
• Style subtitles your way: font, size, color, outline or background, and how high they sit
• Watch together with friends in sync using SyncPlay
• Cast to a Chromecast and use your phone as the remote
• Switch between profiles, each with their own theme, fonts and playback settings
• Sign in with a password or Quick Connect, without typing on the TV
• Set quality limits for Wi-Fi and mobile data separately
• Pick a screensaver for your TV: your library's artwork, a wall of posters, a clock or a floating logo, each with its own options
• Check on your server, scan your libraries and see who's watching, right from Settings

Chameleon is a third-party app made with love for the Jellyfin community. It isn't affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
```
